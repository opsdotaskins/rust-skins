### 2. Information we collect

The information involved depends on how you use the service and the information needed for your Account or transaction:

- **Account and contact information:** your email address, account identifier, login information and contact details you provide. Name, date of birth, country, telephone number or billing address may also be required for eligibility, payment or transaction verification, depending on the relevant form and transaction.
- **Steam and delivery information:** your Steam identifier, profile information made available to us, Steam Trade URL, destination account and relevant trade references, statuses and Item-transfer records.
- **Orders and Account Balance:** Items ordered, amounts, currency, top-ups, balance adjustments, refund requests, payment references and transaction history.
- **Payment-related information:** payment status, authorisation or authentication results and transaction details returned by payment service providers. Depending on the integration, this may include limited card-identification information, such as card network and masked digits, rather than full card credentials.
- **Technical and security information:** IP address, browser and device information, request timestamps, sign-in activity, error records and information reasonably needed to identify misuse or investigate a transaction.
- **Communications:** messages, attachments and information you provide when requesting support, a refund, Account closure or the exercise of a privacy right.
- **Verification information:** information reasonably needed in a particular case to confirm age, identity, Account control, payment authorisation or refund entitlement. If additional documents are needed, we will explain the request and an appropriate submission method. Do not send full card details, security codes, passwords or Steam Guard codes by email.
- **Preferences and storage choices:** relevant settings and choices described in our Cookie Policy.

Information comes from you, your use of the Website, Steam in connection with the relevant account or trade, and service providers involved in a transaction or security check. We do not currently use connected advertising or analytics services on the Website.

### 7. How long we keep information

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose, including applicable legal, accounting, security and claims requirements. Retention depends on the record category:

- **Account information** is kept while needed to administer the Account and, after closure, to settle outstanding matters and retain records lawfully required.
- **Order, top-up, payment, refund and accounting records** are retained for the applicable legal recordkeeping period and for any relevant payment dispute or legal claim.
- **Trade and delivery records** are retained as needed to evidence fulfilment, provide support and resolve related disputes.
- **Support correspondence** is retained according to the nature of the enquiry, follow-up needs and any related claim or transaction.
- **Security logs and verification material** are retained for a period proportionate to the security or verification purpose, any continuing investigation and any applicable legal obligation. Documents are not kept merely because they might be useful indefinitely.
- **Browser storage** remains for the relevant session or period described in the Cookie Policy and the associated settings, unless removed earlier.

When information is no longer required, we delete it or irreversibly anonymise it, subject to the controlled expiry of backup copies and any lawful preservation requirement. Account closure does not necessarily require immediate deletion of transaction records we must retain.
