### 2. Accepted payment methods and currencies

We accept **Visa and Mastercard**. You can make purchases in **EUR, GBP or USD**. Payment options available for the particular transaction are displayed before payment. You may also use available Account Balance where offered at checkout.

Check the transaction currency before authorising payment. If it differs from your card account currency, your card issuer or bank may convert the amount and apply its own exchange rate or charges. Those charges are governed by your agreement with that institution.

Where a balance currency or conversion is relevant, its treatment must be shown before you confirm the transaction. Selecting a display currency does not authorise an undisclosed conversion of an existing balance.

### 3. Prices, taxes and charges

The amount payable, currency and any applicable taxes or Company charges are disclosed before you place the Order or confirm the top-up. Check the final total before paying.

We will not add an undisclosed Company charge after you authorise the transaction. A bank or card issuer may separately apply charges outside our control. If a charge or amount is unclear, contact us before proceeding.

### 4. Payment processing and authentication

Payments are handled through third-party payment service providers. A provider, card issuer or bank may require authentication, authorisation or proportionate fraud checks. Follow the legitimate instructions presented within the payment flow.

You must use a payment method you are authorised to use and provide accurate billing and transaction information. Do not email full card numbers, card security codes or authentication credentials to us.

A payment may be approved, declined, pending, reversed or subject to a temporary authorisation. A reserved amount is not necessarily a completed charge. Delivery or availability of a top-up can depend on confirmation of the payment and completion of necessary checks.

Using an external payment service does not remove our responsibility as seller for an Order, a refund due or accurate Account Balance records. Independent providers may have their own terms and privacy notices for their processing activities.

### 5. Declined, pending or duplicated transactions

If payment is declined, check the details and contact your card issuer where appropriate. Avoid repeated attempts while the result is unclear, as multiple authorisations may appear.

If you appear to have been charged but an Order or top-up is not recorded, email **info@rust-skins.com** with the transaction date, amount, currency and reference. We will reconcile the payment with the relevant provider. We do not require disclosure of full card details to trace a payment.

Confirmed duplicate charges or amounts taken for an Order we cannot accept or supply will be corrected or refunded as appropriate. Release of a temporary authorisation is controlled by the relevant provider and card issuer, and differs from refunding a settled charge.

### 6. Account top-ups

A top-up adds purchase credit to your Account after the relevant payment is confirmed. It does not itself purchase a particular Item, guarantee stock, reserve a price or establish delivery of digital content.

Unused paid balance may be refunded by emailing **info@rust-skins.com**. The detailed rules appear in the [Account Balance & Top-Up Policy](/legal/balance). We do not provide seller payouts or peer-to-peer transfers through this function.

An Order or top-up authorises the amount and payment identified at confirmation. It does not by itself authorise a subscription or recurring debit.

Any transaction limit applicable to your payment should be shown or explained before payment where practicable. We do not impose an undisclosed forfeiture or penalty merely because a payment requires review.

Payments and top-ups are unavailable to customers in the restricted countries identified in our Terms and where other applicable restrictions prevent the transaction. We do not guarantee that a card will be accepted solely because its network is listed here.

### 8. Refunds and payment disputes

Refunds are governed by the [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/legal/refunds). They normally return to the original payment method in the original transaction currency. Card-issuer posting times may differ from the time we submit the refund instruction.

For an Order paid from Account Balance, the reversed amount normally returns to that balance, with unused paid balance refundable to its originating payment method under the relevant policy. This does not override a statutory requirement concerning the refund method.

Contact us promptly if you suspect an unauthorised charge or payment error. You may also contact your bank or card issuer and use any available statutory or card-scheme process. We do not require a waiver of those rights. Where a refund or reversal already resolves the same amount, records will be reconciled to prevent duplicate recovery.
