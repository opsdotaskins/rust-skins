Refund and cancellation requests should be sent to **info@rust-skins.com**. This Policy supplements our [Terms & Conditions](/legal/terms), [Digital Delivery Policy](/legal/delivery) and [Account Balance & Top-Up Policy](/legal/balance).

Nothing in this Policy removes a mandatory consumer right or limits the time allowed by law to bring a claim. Different rules apply to cancelling a digital-content purchase, a problem with delivery and a refund of unused paid balance.

### 2. Statutory cancellation rights

Where applicable law provides a cooling-off right for your distance contract, you may cancel within the relevant period without giving a reason. For UK consumers, the period is generally 14 calendar days from conclusion of a contract for digital content or services, subject to the statutory rules and exceptions. If legally required cancellation information is not provided, the period may be extended by law.

To exercise an applicable right, send a clear statement that you wish to cancel to **info@rust-skins.com**, or write to the Company address in section 1. You may use the optional form in section 11, but it is not compulsory. Any additional online cancellation method made available for your contract may also be used. For a time-limited statutory right, it is sufficient to send your cancellation statement before the applicable period expires.

### 3. Starting digital supply during the cancellation period

Where the statutory digital-content exception applies, we may start supply during the cooling-off period only after obtaining your prior express consent and acknowledgement that you will lose the cooling-off right when supply begins, together with the confirmation required by law.

An ordinary acceptance of the Terms, payment authorisation or Account top-up alone does not satisfy this separate requirement. If the legal conditions for loss of the right have not been met, we will not rely on the digital-content exception to reject a valid cancellation.

Loss of a cooling-off right does not remove remedies for missing delivery, an incorrect Item or content that does not conform to the contract.

### 4. Cancelling an Order before delivery

Contact us promptly if you want to cancel before the Item is transferred. We will check the Order and trade status and whether cancellation is available under the contract or law. Where an eligible cancellation can be completed, we will cancel the pending supply and return the amount due.

If a trade offer is outstanding when cancellation is agreed, do not accept it. We will take the steps available to cancel it. If delivery completes during the request, we will assess the request against the actual timing and applicable rights; the presence of an Order status alone does not determine the outcome.

### 5. Non-delivery, incorrect Items and other problems

Contact us if:

- you paid for an Item that was not delivered;
- you received an Item different from the contractual description;
- only part of an Order was delivered;
- a duplicate or incorrect charge was taken; or
- another failure gives you a remedy under applicable law.

We will examine payment and trade records and, where appropriate, information you provide. Depending on the issue and your legal rights, the remedy may include completing delivery, supplying the correct Item, correcting the charge, a price reduction or a refund. We will not require you to accept a replacement where the law entitles you to a refund.

For partial delivery, a refund will normally cover the affected portion of the Order, without limiting any right to cancel a wider part of the contract. We will not replace a missing Item with a different one without your agreement.

If an Item must be transferred back as part of a lawful remedy, use only instructions confirmed through **info@rust-skins.com** and verify the destination. Do not send an Item to an unverified account. Any return arrangement must be compatible with platform restrictions and your statutory rights.

### 6. Unused paid Account Balance

You may request a refund of unused paid Account Balance by emailing **info@rust-skins.com**. This voluntary refund facility is not limited to 14 days after top-up.

The refundable amount is the unused portion of money actually paid and credited to your Account, taking into account completed purchases, settled refunds and genuine payment reversals. An amount committed to a pending Order must first be reconciled with that Order; you are not required to make a further purchase to recover unused funds.

We may reasonably verify Account control, the original payment and the amount available. A partial use of a top-up does not by itself make the remaining paid balance non-refundable. Inactivity, suspension or Account closure does not by itself cancel the right to a refund.

If a legal prohibition or a genuine unresolved payment dispute prevents immediate repayment, we will explain the position to the extent lawful and resolve the restriction as reasonably possible. Checks will not be used to override a mandatory deadline without a lawful basis.

### 7. Change of mind after completed delivery

Outside an applicable statutory right or another refund entitlement described here, we do not offer a voluntary return solely because you change your mind after the correct Item has been delivered.

An Item's later market-price movement, a change in your cosmetic preference or an unrelated restriction caused by your own later conduct does not by itself create a refund entitlement. These examples do not exclude a claim where we are responsible or applicable law provides a remedy.

### 8. How to request a refund

Email **info@rust-skins.com** with:

- the email associated with your Account;
- your Order or top-up reference and approximate transaction date;
- the amount and currency involved;
- a short description of the issue or a statement that you want unused balance refunded; and
- any relevant trade reference or supporting screenshot.

You do not need to give a reason when exercising a statutory no-reason cancellation right. Do not email full card numbers, card security codes, passwords or Steam Guard codes. We will explain any proportionate verification that is needed.

Our target is to provide an initial response within **five business days**. A business day is Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England and Wales. If an investigation needs more time, we will explain the next steps. These service targets do not extend a statutory cancellation or reimbursement deadline.

### 9. Refund method and timing

Refunds are normally made to the original payment method in the currency of the original transaction. If an Order was paid from Account Balance, its reversal normally restores that balance; any unused paid balance may then be refunded to its originating payment method under section 6. A refund payable by law to an original payment method will not be replaced with store credit without your agreement where such agreement is legally required.

If the original method cannot receive the refund, we will work with you and the payment service provider to arrange a lawful alternative after appropriate verification. We will not direct a refund to an unrelated third party.

For an approved voluntary refund, our target is to submit the refund instruction within **five business days after approval**. Card-issuer processing may affect when it appears in your account. We will provide the available confirmation or reference so the payment can be traced.

Where a statutory deadline applies, we will meet that deadline. In particular, for a valid UK statutory cancellation to which the 14-day reimbursement rule applies, reimbursement is due without undue delay and no later than **14 calendar days after we are informed of the cancellation**, subject only to a legally applicable exception. Our investigation or approval process does not restart that clock. Any earlier deadline required by applicable law also takes priority.

We do not impose a Company administration fee for making a refund under this Policy. Any bank conversion difference is governed by the bank's applicable exchange treatment; this does not reduce an amount we are legally required to repay.

### 10. Unauthorised payments and disputes

Report a suspected unauthorised payment promptly to **info@rust-skins.com** and to your bank or card issuer as appropriate. We will investigate and cooperate with the relevant payment process. Your rights concerning an unauthorised transaction are not conditional on funds remaining in your Account.

You are not required to waive a chargeback, statutory remedy or right to contact a regulator. The same amount cannot be recovered twice: if a refund or reversal has already settled the issue, we will reconcile the records and any related balance accordingly.
