### 2. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small pieces of information stored on your device by a website. Similar technologies include browser local storage and session storage. They can maintain a session, remember a setting or support a function you request.

First-party technologies are set by the website you visit. Third-party technologies are set by another service involved in a website function, such as a payment flow. A technology's legal treatment depends on its purpose and operation, not simply on whether it is called a cookie.

### 4. Duration

Session technologies are intended to last for a browser session or the relevant session context. Persistent settings can remain until their configured expiry, your next update to the setting or deletion through browser controls. Local storage does not necessarily expire automatically and can remain until removed by the Website or by you.

The duration depends on the particular setting and function. You may remove stored information through your browser as explained below. We do not use this Policy to authorise indefinite retention of personal data.

### 5. Your choices

Technologies that require consent must not be activated before you make the relevant affirmative choice. You may refuse non-essential technologies and withdraw an earlier consent through the consent controls made available for those technologies. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier use.

You can also use browser settings to inspect, block or remove cookies and other site data. Browser controls vary, and deleting cookies may not remove local storage unless you also clear site data. Blocking all storage can prevent sign-in, checkout or another function from working. Removing privacy-choice storage may cause the Website to ask for your choices again.

Continued browsing, silence or acceptance of the Terms & Conditions is not consent to technologies that require a separate choice.
